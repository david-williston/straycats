# Stray Cats brand kit (Saibo)

The Stray Cats identity is a portrait of Saibo: charcoal-grey fur, white chest and paws, golden
eyes and a white marking beside the nose, against a sunset skyline. The artwork was generated
with ChatGPT and exists only as raster images (no vector version).

The same files can be downloaded one at a time from the Press page:
https://david-williston.github.io/straycats/press/

## Folders

| Folder | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `01_Artwork/` | Master artwork: the logo (cat and "Straycats" wordmark) on a transparent background (950×789) and on white (1055×805), plus the original concept sheet showing the logo and three icon variations. |
| `02_Web/` | `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48, 64 px), favicon PNGs at those sizes, the logo as WebP, a 1400×1100 hero image, and a 1200×630 link-preview card. |
| `03_App_Icons/` | Square app icons, 72–1024 px, for the home screen (PWA), iOS and Android; maskable 192 and 512 px icons for Android; and the dark and light 1024 px portrait sources. |
| `04_Print/` | The logo on white as a 300 dpi TIFF. |

## Suggested use

- Browser tab: `02_Web/favicon.ico`, with `favicon-32.png` as a PNG fallback.
- iOS home screen: `03_App_Icons/straycats-app-icon-180.png` (`apple-touch-icon`).
- Web app manifest: `straycats-app-icon-192.png` and `-512.png`, plus the maskable 192 and 512.
- Site header and pages: `02_Web/straycats-logo-transparent.webp`, or the PNG in `01_Artwork/`.
- Android adaptive icon background: dark navy `#10161F`.

## Size limits

The artwork's native size is about 1000 px wide. The 1024 px app icon was enlarged from a
smaller source, so it has no more real detail than the 512 px one. The 300 dpi TIFF is the same
pixels as the on-white logo with print metadata, so it prints well only up to about 9 × 7 cm.
Large-format print would need the artwork redrawn at a higher resolution.

The 16 and 32 px favicons are scaled down from the 1024 px portrait icon. At those sizes the
portrait is only a rough shape; that is expected.
