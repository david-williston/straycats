Thank you for checking. I'm glad you stopped.

**If the collar has a phone number, please call or message that person.** They put the collar on
this cat, and they want to hear from the cat's family.

Soon you'll be able to type the cat's ID from the collar here and see whether the cat has been
reported missing. That isn't built yet.

**If you think this is your cat,** please also ask a vet or an animal shelter to scan for a
microchip. There is more in [When a cat goes missing](/ideas/when-a-cat-goes-missing/).

If it's a paper collar, you can read about it, and print your own, on the
[paper collar page](/ideas/paper-collar/).
