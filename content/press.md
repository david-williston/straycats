Writing about StrayCats, or about the people who care for cats? Here's the project in brief, artwork
you're welcome to use, and how to reach me.

## In brief

**StrayCats** is an open-source, non-profit project to make caring for a cat easier, by
letting anyone who cares for one see its story, so nothing is lost when a cat changes hands.
The cat might be your own, a neighbor's, a stray, a new adoption, one you had to give up, or
one left behind when its owner died. It starts with the cats I've come to know in Puerto
Escondido, Mexico, and a simple question: what if every cat had an identity, a history, and
a network of people who care about it, and meeting one took nothing more than a photo?

- **What it is:** a growing open-source project, worked out in the open. The
  [ideas](/ideas/) and [stories](/posts/) on this site keep growing as people share what
  they know, and [What's New](/whats-new/) records each step. An early app is
  [under construction](/app/), in the open.
- **Who's behind it:** David Williston, who looks after a house full of cats in Puerto
  Escondido, Oaxaca, Mexico.
- **Who it's for:** anyone who cares for a cat, whether it's theirs or not.
- **Open source and non-profit:** everything is public on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/david-williston/straycats).
- **The cat comes first:** the cat's wellbeing always comes first, then the people who care
  for cats.
- **Dedicated to [Saibo](/saibo/),** my cat of sixteen years, who died in Puerto Escondido in
  September 2026. The cat in the logo is Saibo.
- **Languages:** English and Spanish.

For more, see [About](/about/), or listen to the
[90-second audio introduction](/about/#listen-to-the-idea).

## Logo and artwork

The logo is a portrait of Saibo resting in front of a sunset over the rooftops, with the
"Straycats" wordmark and a ".love" heart. Click a picture or **Download** to save the full-size file.

The ZIP also holds app icons from 72 to 1024 px, favicons, and a short guide.

### Using the artwork

You're welcome to use the logo and artwork in articles, posts and broadcasts about Stray
Cats. Please don't change the colours, distort it, or separate Saibo from the wordmark.

The artwork was generated with ChatGPT and exists only as raster images (there's no vector
version). It's about 1400 px wide, so it prints sharply only at small sizes. If you need it
larger, get in touch.

## Link to StrayCats

If you run a clinic, a blog or a rescue, you can add a small card to your website that links to the
project. It's free, and it keeps itself up to date. This is the card, live:

{{< straycats-card-demo >}}

To add it, paste this where you'd like it to appear:

```html
<straycats-card>
  <a href="https://straycats.love/">StrayCats project</a>
</straycats-card>
<script src="https://straycats.love/card.js" async></script>
```

For colours, the one-line version and Hugo, see [the full instructions](/embed/).
