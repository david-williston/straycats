The difference between the two: [stories](/posts/) are about things that really happened, to
cats and to the people who care for them. Ideas are what I take from them: concepts and open
questions about how a cat could have one identity and a growing history, how [meeting a
cat](/ideas/meeting-a-cat/) could be the easy way in, and how all of it stays for the cat's
benefit and for the people who care for it.

Ideas are **not requirements or a product plan**, just my current thinking, written down so
you can poke holes in it. If something in the stories doesn't match what I've written here,
[tell me what I'm missing →](/feedback/)
