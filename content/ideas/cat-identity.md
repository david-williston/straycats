*Background: [Ninja, Naranja, or Milo?](/posts/many-names-one-cat/)*

## The idea

Under all the names, there's one **cat**. Every person who meets it makes **claims** about
it: what they call it, where they see it, whether they feed it, whether they think it's
owned. Claims can disagree, and that's fine.

Over time, the system may notice that two records probably describe the **same cat**:
similar photos, same coat pattern, overlapping territory, the same notched ear. It could
suggest merging them, and people would confirm or reject the match.

Things that might help recognise an individual:

- **Photos.** Coat patterns on tabbies, calicos and tuxedos are close to unique.
- **Distinguishing marks**: ear tip, notched ear, tail kink, scars, eye colour.
- **Place and time.** Sightings near each other in time and space.
- **People's knowledge**: "that's definitely Naranja".

## Open questions

- How do you show a cat with several names without picking a "winner"?
- What happens when a merge turns out to be wrong? Can two cats be split apart again?
- How do you tell solid black cats apart?
- Who gets to confirm that two records are the same cat?
