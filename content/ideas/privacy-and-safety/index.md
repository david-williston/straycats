## The concern

A public map of exactly where stray cats live and get fed is useful to helpers — but also
to people who want to harm animals, or who object to colonies in their area. Caretakers'
own locations and routines also need protecting.

## Possible approaches

- **Fuzzed public locations** — show an approximate area, not an exact point.
- **Trusted roles** — verified rescuers/caretakers see precise data.
- **Delayed publishing** — sightings appear publicly after a delay.
- **Minimal personal data** — collect as little about users as possible.

## Open questions

- Where's the right balance between openness and safety?
- Who decides who is "trusted"?
