Been here before? Here's what's new and what's changed, newest first. Pages that changed
also say **New** or **Updated** under their title.

## November 20, 2026 (newest): Print your own paper collar

*Version v2026.11.20.3*

- **New:** a free paper collar template you can [print at home](/ideas/paper-collar/#print-your-own)
  on plain paper, in English or Spanish, A4 or US Letter. Each strip asks "Is this your cat?
  Please call me." and has space for your phone number and the cat's ID, plus a QR code.
  Short instructions explain how to put it on safely.
- **New:** a [page for anyone who finds a cat](/c/) wearing a StrayCats collar. The QR code
  leads there.

## November 20, 2026 (latest): Why NFC, and why open source

*Version v2026.11.20.2*

- **Updated:** [What If Every Cat Could Carry an Open Identity?](/ideas/open-cat-identity/)
  now explains why phones can't read implanted microchips but can read NFC tags, what happens
  when someone taps a cat's collar with their phone, and how you could program a sheet of cheap
  paper collars yourself. It also says why StrayCats is open source: so anyone can check it and
  help improve it, on one shared platform that no company controls.
- **New:** StrayCats, its code and this website alike, is now licensed under the
  [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/david-williston/straycats/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **Updated:** the [paper collar specification](/ideas/paper-collar-spec/) links to the reasons
  for NFC.

## November 20, 2026 (later): A debate about the paper collar specification

*Version v2026.11.20.1*

- **Updated:** [The Paper Collar: a First Specification](/ideas/paper-collar-spec/) now opens
  with what it's for: one open, worldwide specification for cat collars, from a disposable strip
  for a stray to a durable collar for your own cat. It says plainly that this first draft was
  written with AI and is probably wrong in many ways, to get people talking. You can also listen
  to an AI-generated debate about its details (about 19 minutes, in English).

## November 20, 2026: A first specification for the paper collar

*Version v2026.11.20*

- **New:** [The Paper Collar: a First Specification](/ideas/paper-collar-spec/), a draft
  design for a ready-made collar: reversible, water-resistant, with a breakaway weak link, a
  one-way ratchet and an NFC tag that carries only an ID. It has sizes from kitten to large,
  force targets, a test plan and open questions. Nothing is built or tested yet.
- **Updated:** [The Paper Collar](/ideas/paper-collar/) links to the new specification.
- **Updated:** drawings on the site can now be tapped to open large, like photos.

## November 13, 2026 (night): A cat's story, and who it might be

*Version v2026.11.13.3*

- **In the app:** when you meet a cat in a place where you've met one before, the app asks
  "This might be Ninja. Is it?", and you always decide. A cat's story can now hold the big
  moments of its life: born, went missing, found, sterilised, passed away, each with a date as
  rough as you know it ("around 2019"). If two entries disagree, like two different dates for
  the same death, the app gently points it out and leaves it to people to sort out; it never
  decides who's right. You can also sign out and switch accounts. See the new photo in
  [The App, Under Construction](/app/).

  cat under their own name; nothing is merged. See the new photos in
  [The App, Under Construction](/app/).
