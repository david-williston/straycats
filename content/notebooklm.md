This page holds the full text of the Stray Cats website in English, one page after another,
for use as a source in NotebookLM. Stray Cats is an open-source, non-profit idea, still at
the concept stage, about how the world could remember the cats nobody owns. It's written by
David Williston, who looks after a house full of cats in Puerto Escondido, Mexico. There is no
app yet. The site is at https://david-williston.github.io/straycats/.
