This page holds the full text of the StrayCats website in English, one page after another,
for use as a source in NotebookLM. StrayCats is an open-source, non-profit project, growing
in the open, about how the world could remember the cats nobody owns. It's written by
David Williston, who looks after a house full of cats in Puerto Escondido, Mexico. There is no
app or code yet. The site is at https://straycats.love/.
